Your training record lives in your own OneDrive — saved under your @phoenix.gov Microsoft account, in this app's private folder. You sign in as yourself, your entries back up to your account, and no one else can see them — not other firefighters, not the person who shared this page with you.

  • One record file, in your OneDrive app folder (training-record.json) — it follows your account to any device.
  • Send each month's report to your training officer straight from the app, from your own mailbox.
First-time setup — connect this page to Microsoft (one time)

This page talks directly to Microsoft Graph — there's no shared server. It needs its own (free) app registration so Microsoft knows it's allowed to ask for sign-in:

  1. Go to portal.azure.com → App registrations → New registration. Name it Probationary Training Record, and choose Accounts in any organizational directory.
  2. Under Redirect URI, pick Single-page application (SPA) and enter the exact web address of this page.
  3. Open the app → API permissions → Add a permission → Microsoft Graph → Delegated, and add: User.Read, Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder, and Mail.Send. (If sign-in later fails with a permissions error, a city IT admin may need to grant consent for these.)
  4. Copy the Application (client) ID from the app's Overview page and paste it here. It's stored only in this browser, only to configure sign-in.
